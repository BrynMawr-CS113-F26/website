1. Library

A library stores many kinds of items. A general library item has a title and an identification number. A book is a special kind of library item that also has an author.

Create three files in the your /lab8 directory: LibraryItem.java, Book.java, and Main.java. You can use the touch command to do this. For example: touch Book.java.
